Professional Statement — Daniella Dougall
My path into cybersecurity is rooted in over nine years of experience
as a licensed entrepreneur and healthcare professional. As the
founder and owner of D’Lash-Us, I have built and sustained a thriving
beauty business from the ground up, managing client relationships,
business operations, and the trust that comes with serving people in
an intimate, detail-oriented space. Alongside this, my work as a
licensed massage therapist specializing in post-operative care has
sharpened my ability to operate with precision, discretion, and a deep
commitment to the wellbeing of those I serve.
These experiences have taught me that protecting people is not just a
professional obligation, it is a core value I carry into every area of my
work.
Now, I am channeling that same dedication into the world of
technology. Having completed a Coding for Beginners certification, I
have developed foundational skills in languages including Python,
SQL, HTML, CSS, JavaScript, C#, and Ruby, and I have begun
building real-world projects including data analytics dashboards, AI
agent integrations, and full-stack web applications. I am driven by a
long-term vision of developing my own software products: including
platforms designed to support entrepreneurs and streamline access to
legal and business resources.
Pursuing cybersecurity is a natural and necessary next step in that
journey. I understand that any application I build must be secure by
design, and I want to be the kind of developer who does not just
create powerful tools, but protects the people who use them. My goal
is to bring together my background in client trust, business ownership,
and emerging technical expertise to contribute meaningfully to the
security of the systems and communities I serve.
